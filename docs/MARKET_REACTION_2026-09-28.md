# Does the market lag a new reading? One-minute prices around each new daily maximum (28 Sep 2026)

## Read this first

- **Quoted, not executable.** The price is the venue's one-minute price series; the ask adds the archived books' median half-spread (hourly books), and the fee is the venue's taker fee.
- **t0 is the observation time.** The reading is published some minutes after it: a move before t0 plus a few minutes means the market has faster sources than the published report; a move after it is the lag.
- **Not the platform's speed.** The tick runs hourly; this says whether a faster path would be worth building, not what the platform can do today.
