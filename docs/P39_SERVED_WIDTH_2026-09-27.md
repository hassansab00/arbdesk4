## What it means (written by hand, 27 Sep; not generated)

- **The width served around the corrected centre is too wide.** P3.9 replaced the day-ahead centre and kept the width fitted to the raw forecast's errors. The corrected combination's own out-of-sample error calls for about 1.05 C. The width served was 1.48-1.64 C from 17 Sep, wider before (14 Sep: 6.1 C). For 28 Sep's 44 day-ahead markets, priced 27 Sep 16:36Z, it averages 1.531 C, from 0.515 to 2.766 (`band_probabilities`, queried 27 Sep ~17:05Z). Its 80% interval held 92.8% of outcomes over all scored dates; the fitted widths hold 80-83%.
- **On dates like today's (17-25 Sep), every fitted width beats the served one** on the venue ladder: +0.10 to +0.15 log loss per city-day, each 90% interval above zero, and top-1 hit 39.7% -> about 42%. For scale, P3.9's centre change gained about 0.19 on its go-live replay.
- **The simplest well-scoring candidate is W2**: a per-city width shrunk to the pool, one number per city and lead. It gains +0.138 [+0.105, +0.171]. W3 (EMOS, with the sources' spread) scores a little better at +0.154 with two fitted parameters. Whether that difference is real was not tested.

**Caveats.**
- 9 and 13 dates. Cities and checkpoints of a date are kept together in the bootstrap.
- The scored dates overlap the P3.9 go-live replay, which chose the centre. The widths themselves were fixed before any score was seen.
- The forecasts are Open-Meteo Previous Runs at lead 1, not the live runs the engine reads.
- Since 27 Sep 08:23Z the served centre is the blend with the station model (P2.9), which is a little more accurate; its own width was not measured here.
- The probability served is the raw one this study scores. The calibration map is not applied: `settings.calibration_map` has `applies` false, with the gate unmet at 15 settlement dates of 30. All 6,402 `band_probabilities` rows of the 24 h before ~17:25Z on 27 Sep have `calibrated_prob` = `raw_prob`. Its last fit (26 Sep 05:16Z) found T = 2.0, "too peaked", on ladders priced from the raw centre before P3.9. Once the map passes its gate it scales whatever width is served, so it has to be fitted on that width.
- Under the evaluation contract (plan v2.3 P7.3), a width is promoted on dates after its design froze. These dates are before it.
