## Decision-time hit rates (B1, a pre-P4 proxy for A9)

Venue-resolved ladders with `resolution_date` 13–21 Sep (406 city-days). At
each local-clock checkpoint:
- the **model top** is the band with the highest `calibrated_prob`, from each band's newest price in the 6 h up to the checkpoint;
- the **market top** is the band with the highest mid (`best_ask` when the mid is null), from each band's newest book in the 3 h up to the checkpoint.

Only ladders where every band had both a price and a book count.

| checkpoint (local) | city-days | both complete | model hit % | market hit % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| d1_eve (18:00, day before) | 406 | 304 | **28.6** | **41.8** |
| day_start (00:00) | 406 | 324 | **27.5** | **45.1** |
| morning (09:00) | 406 | 346 | **25.4** | **46.8** |
| noon (12:00) | 406 | 330 | **23.3** | **52.4** |

Ranking by `raw_prob` instead of `calibrated_prob` gave the same hit rate at
every checkpoint.

The audit's noon figures (22.5% model, 53.7% market) came from a method that
is not in the repo. This query's lookback windows are its own choice, so the
two differ slightly. P4 replaces this proxy with checkpoint rows written at
decision time, and those become the numbers to beat. The pre-peak checkpoints
need `derived_weather_peak` and are left to P4.2.
