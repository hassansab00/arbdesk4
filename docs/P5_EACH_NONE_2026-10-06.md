## What each strategy saw

- **The same inputs.** The three strategies decided on the same 1,788 `prediction_checkpoints` rows, one decision each, 5,364 in all. Every row's ladder and book name the same buckets, and every book quotes every bucket.
- **The prior everywhere.** Every one of the 5,364 decisions records the prior for each learned part: `market_anchor` w = 0.0 in all five scopes, and the belief table, λ and h at their priors.
- **So the view is the market's mids.**
  - The view is the book's mids, normalised (`market_anchor.market_probs`: a bucket with only an ask counts at half its ask; one with only a bid, halfway from its bid to 1).
  - The posterior then clips each bucket to [0.001, 0.999] and renormalises (`belief.ladder_posterior`, no table).
  - The model's own probabilities have no weight at w = 0.
- **How the mids sum.** Mid sums ran from 0.9005 to 1.2135 (median 1.0095).
  - A sum above 1 understates every bucket, the leader most in absolute terms.
  - A sum below 1 overstates every bucket.
  - That is the only source of the edges below.

**The edge test.** A YES edge is posterior minus cost; a NO edge is (1 − posterior) minus the cost of NO at 1 − bid.
- Cost is the engine's fee-inclusive price, `price + 0.05 · price · (1 − price)` (`allocator.effective_cost`, what `holdings_solver` sizes on).
- With the plan's worst-case fee (`engine_orders.worst_fee_per_share`), the counts are 14 YES and 249 NO, against 16 and 255 below.

| | YES edge | NO edge |
|---|---|---|
| checkpoints with one | 16 of 1,788 | 255 of 1,788 |
| largest | 0.0265 | 0.1222 |

## Questions for Hassan (no rule was changed)

1. **The market prior manufactures NO edges on the leader.**
   - When a book's mids sum above 1 (wide or one-sided tails), normalising understates the leader. After the peak, the 0.999 clip and renormalisation put a 99.8¢ leader at about 0.99.
   - So NO at 0.1-0.6¢ looks cheap. This happened on 255 of 1,788 checkpoints in the window.
   - The engine stopped all but one: by dead-bucket rules (87), the gate (18), and the band after robust sizing (148). The one it bought (Seattle) lost.
   - Possible changes: normalising differently (two-sided quotes only, or no clip for the market prior), or leaving it to WXPredict, which replaces this prior. Either is a rule change.
2. **The against-market gate names the wrong favourite when the leader is bought up.**
   - The gate takes the market's favourite as the highest ask.
   - All 36 `against_market` decisions in the window (18 s11_ladder, 18 s12) were books whose leader had a bid of at least 0.991 and no ask. So the "favourite" was a bucket at 0.9¢ or less, and the gate fired on a disagreement that is not there.
   - Not trading was right on all 36, for another reason. A fallback to the highest bid or mid when the leader has no ask would be a rule change.
3. **S10 never decides Madrid after its peak.**
   - rd1 is fitted for local hours 7-17 only, and Madrid's `postpeak_1h` falls at 18:xx local, so it was skipped on 7 of 7 days.
   - The fix is a refit, or a stated hour mapping, with its own test. D8 says the engine strategies run as they are until G5, so this waits for Hassan's word.
   - **Resolved 6 Oct.** Hassan: S10 must decide before and during each city's own peak. Measured 29 Sep - 6 Oct, Madrid's post-peak at 18:xx was the only checkpoint outside 7-17.
     - rd1's late hour 18 is fitted beside the served fit, under its own version `rd1:2026-09-25:66d6600c72` (`data/models/remaining_day/late_hours.json`).
     - Walk-forward over 15,695 city-days: log loss 0.122 against the floor-atom proxy's 1.491, a gain of 1.369 (95% 1.318-1.421).
     - The served fit, its hours 7-17 and its version are untouched, so Challenger C's forward comparison, which selects rd1's rows by version, is unchanged.

## Queries

The edge test, per checkpoint. The window CTE is the only thing to change to re-run it.

```sql
with d as (
  select decision_id, strategy_id, checkpoint_id, action, reason_code from public.decisions
  where strategy_id in ('s11_ladder','s11_lock','s12_no')
    and decided_at >= timestamptz '2026-10-06 09:00:00+00' - interval '7 days'
    and decided_at <  timestamptz '2026-10-06 09:00:00+00'),
cp as (select distinct checkpoint_id from d),
b as (select c.checkpoint_id, k band, nullif(c.market->k->>'bid','')::float8 bid, nullif(c.market->k->>'ask','')::float8 ask
      from public.prediction_checkpoints c join cp using (checkpoint_id), jsonb_object_keys(c.probs) k),
m as (select *, case when ask is not null and bid is not null then (ask+bid)/2 when ask is not null then ask/2
                     when bid is not null then (bid+1)/2 end mid,
             case when bid is null and ask is null then 'NO_BOOK' when ask is not null and ask <= 0.02 then 'DEAD_LOSER'
                  when bid is not null and bid >= 0.96 then 'DEAD_WINNER' else 'OK' end st from b),
n as (select *, mid / nullif(sum(mid) over (partition by checkpoint_id),0) pmk from m),          -- market_probs
a as (select *, greatest(pmk, 1e-6) / sum(greatest(pmk, 1e-6)) over (partition by checkpoint_id) pa from n),  -- anchor, w = 0
p as (select *, least(greatest(pa,0.001),0.999) pc from a),                                       -- posterior, prior
q as (select *, pc / sum(pc) over (partition by checkpoint_id) post,
             case when ask > 0 and ask < 1 then ask + 0.05*ask*(1-ask) end cy,                    -- effective_cost(YES)
             case when bid > 0 and bid < 1 then (1-bid) + 0.05*(1-bid)*bid end cn from p)         -- effective_cost(NO at 1 - bid)
select checkpoint_id, count(*) filter (where post > cy) yes_edges, count(*) filter (where 1 - post > cn) no_edges,
       max(post - cy) yes_best, max((1 - post) - cn) no_best, sum(mid) mid_sum
from q group by checkpoint_id;
```

**The rest of the queries.** Each builds on the CTEs above:
- **By the live decision:** join `d` to the per-checkpoint result on `checkpoint_id`, and count by strategy, action and `reason_code`. An edge "on its side" is YES for s11 and NO for s12.
- **What s12 and s11_ladder may buy:**
  - NO when `st = 'OK'` and `bid >= 0.03`;
  - YES when `st = 'OK'` and the ask is between 0.07 and 0.97;
  - the gate removes NO on the highest-ask bucket (s12), or every YES but it (s11_ladder), whenever that bucket is not the highest-posterior one.
- **The λ-Kelly estimate:**
  - f* = (p − c)/(1 − c), and the stake s = min(0.25 f*, 0.03);
  - the gain is p·ln(1 + s(1/c − 1)) + (1 − p)·ln(1 − s).
- **The lock:** `sum(cy)` per checkpoint where every bucket has an ask.

- **s2, per decision:**

```sql
with d as (
  select distinct city_key, resolution_date, decided_at from public.decisions
  where strategy_id = 's2_combination_arb'
    and decided_at >= timestamptz '2026-10-06 09:00:00+00' - interval '7 days'
    and decided_at <  timestamptz '2026-10-06 09:00:00+00'),
bands as (select d.*, b.band_id from d
          join public.v_canonical_markets m on m.city_key = d.city_key and m.resolution_date = d.resolution_date
          join public.v_canonical_bands b on b.market_id = m.market_id),
book as (select x.*, s.best_bid, s.best_ask from bands x left join lateral (
           select bs.best_bid, bs.best_ask from public.book_snapshots bs
           where bs.band_id = x.band_id and bs.observed_at <= x.decided_at
           order by bs.observed_at desc limit 1) s on true)
select city_key, resolution_date, decided_at, count(*) n,
       bool_and(best_ask > 0 and best_ask < 1) yes_complete, sum(best_ask + 0.05*best_ask*(1-best_ask)) yes_cost,
       bool_and(best_bid > 0 and best_bid < 1) no_complete, sum((1-best_bid) + 0.05*(1-best_bid)*best_bid) no_cost  -- against n - 1
from book group by 1, 2, 3;
```

  The tick cross-check is the same sums over `prediction_checkpoints.market`, with `decided_at` in the window.

**The two robust traces.** These are run with the repository's own modules on the inputs above:
- `engine_shadow.engine_book`, `engine_views.engine_input('s12_no', ctx)`, `engine_shadow.ledger`, `decision_engine.decide(view, book, ledger, params={"clusters": None})`;
- then `holdings_solver.solve_book` at α = 0.25 and α = 1, with `fractional` at λ = 0.25 and the city-day rail.

The inputs are:
- the books: `prediction_checkpoints.market`;
- the bucket bounds: `bands`;
- the floors and ledgers: `ingest_log.detail.engine.inputs` of the run that each decision names.
